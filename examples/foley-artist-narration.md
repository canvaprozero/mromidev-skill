# Narasi demo — how it feels to be a foley artist

## Struktur yang ditiru dari contoh museum (13 baris, second person)

1. Hook = paradoks satu kalimat (pekerjaan yang harus menghilang).
2. Detail fisik konkret + indra (bukan penjelasan abstrak).
3. Masalah terlihat, lalu "diperbaiki" tapi memunculkan masalah baru.
4. Pertanyaan "Wait, why..." di tengah — membalik sudut pandang.
5. "You see," + rule reveal — aturan yang menjelaskan hook.
6. Solusi fisik, verifikasi lewat "lensa" (di sini: headphone/mic).
7. Close dingin, tanpa CTA — yang dibuat justru yang tidak terlihat.

## Narasi (13 baris, 179 kata, ~60 dtk)

You spend all day making sounds for things that never happened.
On the table, a pair of old shoes waits beside a tray of gravel.
You press the heel down once, and the gravel answers too loud.
“Can we hear the footsteps now?” someone asks from the booth.
You try the shoes on carpet instead. The steps vanish, but now the coat rustles.
You hold the coat up to the mic. Every seam crackles like dry leaves.
Wait, why does a sound feel real from one distance and fake from another?
You see, the ear believes the room, not the object. A footstep is only a footstep if the floor agrees.
You build a little room out of blankets and stand the mic exactly where the camera would stand.
Then you walk the scene again, slowly, matching each step to the actor's breath.
This time, you hear the gravel, the coat, and the breath between the steps.
You press record, then take off the headphones for one last listen.
Without them, the room is silent. Every sound in the film was never there.
